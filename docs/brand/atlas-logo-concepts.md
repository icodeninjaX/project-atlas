## Shared brand brief

- Use case: `logo-brand`
- Asset type: symbol-only app logo for ATLAS
- Product: a private personal operating system unifying money, debts, tasks,
  goals, career, and weekly direction into one clear daily route
- Style: precise vector-like mark, minimal flat geometry, strong silhouette,
  readable at 16 px, 32 px, and app-icon scale
- Palette: ATLAS primary blue `#2867E8` on a genuinely transparent background
- Constraints: original symbol only; one-color capable; no text, letters, `A`,
  wordmark, tagline, mockup, container tile, 3D, shadows, watermark, or fine
  details

## Option 1 — Unified Wayfinder

Create four smooth, substantial directional forms converging around one small
central waypoint, producing a compact rounded compass/coordinate silhouette
without drawing a conventional compass rose. The convergence should communicate
many areas of life becoming one clear direction. Avoid a generic compass needle,
map pin, mountain, globe, human figure, or copied tech-logo aesthetic.

## Option 2 — Life Route

Create a single bold continuous path that bends through three purposeful turns
and resolves at a distinct destination point. The negative space should quietly
suggest progress and orientation without becoming a literal letter. The result
should feel calm and decisive. Avoid location pins, road signs, arrows,
checkmarks, mazes, mountains, and globes.

## Option 3 — System Core

Create five rounded modular planes or blocks interlocking radially around a
crisp open center, expressing money, tasks, goals, career, and reflection
operating as one system. Use balanced negative space and make the mark feel
stable and organized. Avoid flowers, gears, medical crosses, generic app grids,
or Microsoft/Slack-like geometry.

## Option 4 — Meridian Overview

Create three bold asymmetric orbital arcs cradling one central waypoint, with a
deliberate open gap pointing upward. Express perspective, interconnected life
areas, and room to move forward without drawing a literal planet. Avoid globe
grids, atoms, eyes, targets, radar, Saturn, and compass needles.

## Option 5 — Open Horizon

Create two sturdy nested forms that make an open gateway or horizon around a
rising central route. Communicate grounded overview, a clear next move, and
forward momentum with abstract geometric forms. Avoid mountains, sunrises,
houses, shields, map pins, literal upward arrows, compass needles, and globes.
